My worldview is in perpetual flux. To love a person is to keep trying to know them, to listen to their changes, to resist the urge to compress them into a static object. To love the world is the same.

I am a musician, which I take as a duty to be an envoy of the muses, and to carry the communal responsibility to animate creative life & thought.

I want to see people freed from any myth that singing requires something they do not have. I want to live in a future where people more commonly sing together at the dinner table and in their living rooms.

I currently work as the Artistic Director of Aurora Chorus.

My north stars are honesty, wisdom, contribution, mastery, and growth; it is by these values that I navigate.
