Melodic Association Index

I built this tool to help myself & my students. Maybe it can help you too.

Type scale degrees (5 1 3), solfège (sol do mi), or tap the keys to find songs that begin that way. Use b3, #4 for chromatic notes. Use 5, to indicate a lower octave, and 5' to indicate a higher octave.

This is a work in progress :-) Let me know if you encounter any issues, or have song suggestions to add!
