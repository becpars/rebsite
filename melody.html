Melody Search

I built this tool to help myself & my students. Maybe it can help you too.

Type scale degrees, solfège, or tap the keys to find songs that begin that way. Use b3, #4 for chromatic notes. Use commas after a number to indicate a lower octave, and apostrophes to indicate a higher octave.

This is a work in progress :-) Let me know if you encounter any issues, or have song suggestions to add!
